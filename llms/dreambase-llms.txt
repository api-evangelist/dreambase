# Dreambase

> Dreambase is an AI-native analytics platform for Supabase. AI data agents connect to your Supabase project, analyze your data, and produce live dashboards, automated reports, and business insights — so teams can build a data-driven company without hiring a data team or standing up a data warehouse.

Key things to know about Dreambase:

- Dreambase connects to a Supabase project in one click via Supabase Auth, with automatic schema detection. Supabase remains the single source of truth; there is no separate data warehouse.
- AI agents handle data engineering, analysis, and insight generation: an Analyst Agent builds and configures dashboards, ad-hoc reports enrich Supabase data with external sources, and Supabase Health Assessments scan projects for security, performance, and reliability issues with recommended fixes.
- Dashboards are production-ready and live: drag-and-drop editing, real-time filtering across datasets, scheduled data refresh, intelligent query caching, and daily KPI notifications via email and Slack.
- Reports support portrait/landscape layouts, a full chart library, shareable live links, automatic email delivery, and PDF/PNG export.
- Data sources beyond Postgres are integrated through a plugin marketplace: MCP tools and REST APIs flow into cached datasets, and custom Agent Skills keep combined sources consistent.
- Plans: Free ($0, 1 user, 1 dashboard), Pro ($29–$49/mo, 3 users), Team ($249/mo annual, 5 users), and Enterprise (custom). Details at https://dreambase.com/pricing
- The product runs at https://app.dreambase.ai (sign up: https://app.dreambase.ai/auth/register). The company site is https://dreambase.com. Note: getdreambase.com is NOT affiliated with Dreambase.
